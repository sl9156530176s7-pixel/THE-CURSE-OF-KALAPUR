Security & Player Trust

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 • Studio: Strange Creation (The Curse of Kalapur v2.4.0)

1. Zero Unnecessary Data Collection

Strange Creation does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personally identifiable information such as your name, home address, email address, phone number, IP tracking profiles, or third-party advertising identifiers.

2. Zero Sensitive Device Permissions

The Curse of Kalapur never requests camera access, microphone recording, contacts, SMS, call logs, or GPS location permissions. Gameplay operates strictly inside your browser's standard WebGL and Web Audio sandbox.

3. Local Device Storage & Offline Caching

The Progressive Web App (PWA) stores only non-sensitive gameplay data locally on your device:

  • Saved game checkpoints and day counter progress in localStorage.
  • Audio volume, horror song toggle, graphics quality preset, and look/movement sensitivity preferences.
  • Static application files, 3D textures, icons, and audio cached by service-worker.js for fast loading and offline single-player support.

4. HTTPS Security & Safe Updates

All application code is delivered exclusively over HTTPS via GitHub Pages. Strange Creation never distributes third-party APK binaries, executable installers, or unverified background scripts. Software updates are verified directly through your browser's Service Worker cache manifest and version.json.

5. Developer & Contact Information

Published by Strange Creation (Founder & Creator: Soham Dnyaneshwar Lokhande). For privacy inquiries, security disclosures, or bug reports, open an issue on our official repository at github.com/sl9156530176s7-pixel/THE-CURSE-OF-KALAPUR.
